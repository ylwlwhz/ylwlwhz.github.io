About简介

I am a senior undergraduate at Zhili College, Tsinghua University, majoring in Mathematics & Physics. My research lives in AI for Math: getting large language models to write proofs that a computer can check line by line in Lean 4.

Lately I have been asking a simple question — when one step of a long proof breaks, why throw away everything else? Our ICML 2026 paper makes proof sketches editable, so a prover repairs only what failed and keeps every lemma it has already verified. I now lead a Beijing NSF student project that pushes this idea toward long, multi-lemma proofs.

Outside formal math, I enjoy the messy geometry of embodied AI — making robot data from many sources agree on where things actually are.

我是清华大学致理书院数理基础科学专业的大四本科生，研究方向是 AI for Math：让大语言模型写出能在 Lean 4 中被计算机逐行验证的数学证明。

最近我一直在想一个朴素的问题：长证明里只有一步出错时，为什么要把其余部分全部推倒重来？我们发表在 ICML 2026 的工作让证明草图变得可编辑，证明器只修复出错的部分，已经验证过的引理全部保留。现在我作为学生负责人主持一项北京市自然科学基金项目，把这一思路推向长程、多引理的证明。

形式化数学之外，我也喜欢具身智能里那些"凌乱的几何问题"——让来自不同来源的机器人数据，对物体究竟在哪里达成一致。

Editable proof sketches for theorem proving基于可编辑证明草图的自动定理证明

Editable Proof Sketch for Automated Theorem Proving

Most proof sketches are immutable: one failed subgoal and the whole sketch is rebuilt, discarding lemmas that were already proved. We make the sketch editable — failed subgoals are rewritten in place, hard ones are decomposed further, and every verified node is kept.

现有证明草图大多不可编辑：一个子目标出错，整张草图就要重建，已证明的引理也随之丢弃。我们让草图可以就地编辑：出错的子目标原地改写，过难的继续分解，已验证的节点全部保留。

My part: the parallel proving agent workflow, and the core comparison experiments and evaluation.

我的工作：开发支持并行证明的 Agent 工作流，主导核心对比实验与性能评估。

Autoformalizing long, multi-lemma proofs面向长程多引理结构的大模型自动形式化

Beijing NSF Youth Student Project · Tsinghua "Qiyan" Program北京市自然科学基金青年学生项目 · 清华大学"启研"计划

Three threads: a benchmark of long proofs with explicit lemma-dependency DAGs, a lemma-topology-aware autoformalization model, and an end-to-end workflow that repairs proofs iteratively. The benchmark is on track, with a first round of evaluation on mainstream models done.

三条主线：构建带引理依赖 DAG 的长程证明评测基准、训练引理拓扑感知的自动形式化模型、搭建可迭代修复的端到端工作流。评测基准建设按计划推进，已完成部分主流模型的首轮评测。

A Glimpse of Training VLMs as Foundation Models for Robotic Manipulation

My part: coordinate conventions and camera alignment. I unified reference frames, gripper axes, pose formats and end-effector reference points across four heterogeneous data sources, and re-calibrated camera extrinsics by projecting the robot model into the image wherever released extrinsics were missing or off.

我的工作：坐标约定与相机对齐。统一四个异构数据源的参考系、夹爪轴、位姿表示与末端参考点；对外参缺失或不准的数据集，把机器人模型按关节状态投影到图像，重新标定主相机外参。
